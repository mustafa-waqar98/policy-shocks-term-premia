Is the Fed moving the 10-year through rate expectations or through the term premium?

Every Treasury yield is split each day into the average short rate the market expects and the term premium it demands for duration risk. FOMC surprises, measured from futures in a 30-minute window around each statement, are traced through both.

02

FOMC shocks

A hawkish surprise that sends stocks down is a monetary policy shock. A hawkish surprise that sends stocks up is an information shock: the market reads the Fed as more upbeat on the economy (Jarocinski and Karadi 2020).

06

How it works

Term premium

Five principal components of the curve follow a VAR. Regressing bond excess returns on factor shocks and lagged factors gives the market price of each risk. Pricing bonds with those prices set to zero gives expected rates; the rest is term premium.

FOMC shocks

Futures-implied rates are measured 10 minutes before and 20 minutes after each statement. The common move is the policy surprise; it is split into target and path, and into policy and information by the reaction of stocks.

Transmission

For each horizon, the change in an outcome since the day before the meeting is regressed on the shocks. Plotting the slope against the horizon traces how each shock works through expectations and the term premium.
